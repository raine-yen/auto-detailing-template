## What's Missing (Opportunities)
1. **NO dedicated website** — currently on free Wix (pepedagreat.wix.com) — no custom domain, no online booking
2. **No ceramic coating service** listed — but competitors all offer it (XPEL, Detailership, Aegis at $600-$2,000 range in Livermore)
3. **No online booking** — customers must call/text, which is a friction point
4. **No before/after gallery** — competitors showcase transformations; Pepe's has 117 five-star reviews but no visual proof
5. **No SEO** — his Wix URL `pepedagreat.wix.com/pepesmobiledetail` is unoptimized; he ranks on Google but has no organic web presence
6. **No subscription/membership model** — competitors like Andrew's Automotive (TN) offer subscription plans; Livermore commuters could benefit
7. **No fleet services** — Livermore has labs (LLNL), wineries, and businesses with fleets
8. **No social proof integration** — 117 Yelp reviews worth millions in trust, but not showcased on his site
9. **No payment integration** — customers just call/text with no estimate or booking flow
10. **No specialization branding** — Tesla specialists get premium pricing; his Tesla reviews are gold

## Unique Selling Points to Highlight
1. **"Detailing Done Right at Your Doorstep"** — his actual tagline
2. **117 five-star Yelp reviews** — more than most competitors with shops
3. **Solo operator = personal attention** — neighbors recommend "Andrew" by name
4. **Mobile convenience** — works while you're at work, at the gym, visiting family
5. **Affordable** — positioned as $/economical in a market where competitors charge $200-$2,000+
6. **Versatile** — cars, trucks, boats, RVs, motorcycles (most competitors do cars only)
7. **Bring own water** — no need for customer to provide utilities (big advantage for mobile)
8. **Tesla specialists** — multiple reviews from Tesla owners who "don't trust anyone else"
9. **Family-oriented** — his Wix says "Fun, energetic, family oriented company"
10. **Last-minute availability** — Yelp review mentions "was able to come last minute"

## Recommended Website Features for Pepe's
1. **Prominent phone number** with click-to-call on mobile
2. **Before/after draggable gallery** — he has 5 photos on Yelp, can be expanded
3. **Service packages** with clear pricing tiers (economy/full/premium)
4. **Yelp review integration** — show 5.0★, 117 reviews prominently
5. **"How It Works"** — book → we come → you relax (simple 3-step flow)
6. **Vehicle type selector** — car, truck, SUV, boat, RV, motorcycle
7. **Subscription/membership tier** — monthly maintenance plans for commuters
8. **Service area map** — Livermore, Pleasanton, Dublin, San Ramon, Alamo
9. **Online booking CTA** — "Book Your Detail" button everywhere
10. **Personal brand** — "Andrew" not "Pepe's" as the face; family-oriented messaging
